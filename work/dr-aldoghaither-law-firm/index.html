Legal clarity. Confident decisions.

WHAT IT IS

Saudi legal services / law firm

A Saudi law firm presenting legal advisory and representation services to individuals and businesses, with emphasis on corporate, investment, competition, real-estate/construction, enforcement, estate-liquidation and legal-department operations matters.

This case study documents what the linked website presents. Individual contribution to this project has not been confirmed.

WHAT THE WEBSITE OFFERS

  • Arabic RTL homepage with a firm introduction, service cards, team profiles, knowledge content, news/social updates and contact information.
  • Hero section presents the firm as a specialized Saudi legal company, with a Riyadh skyline image, consultation/service CTAs and counters. The counters visibly animate and differ between captures, so treat the figures as displayed claims rather than independently verified results.
  • Partner/client logo display and supporting copy say the firm serves public, private and nonprofit entities; examples of visible logos include stc, Ministry of Health and Al Obeikan Investment Group.
  • Six service cards are visibly listed: legal-department operations product, corporate/investment matters, competition-law violations, real estate/construction, enforcement of judgments, and estate liquidation. Opening the first card displays a scope modal with operational setup, governance, procedures and performance-measurement elements.
  • The firm/about section describes a four-step working method: understand/evaluate, build strategy, execute/follow up, then report/measure.
  • AI legal-assistant section shows a question text area, sample prompt about objecting to White Land Fees, a required processing-consent statement, and a button labeled «Arabic text Arabic text Arabic text Arabic text». Copy describes the answer as preliminary and not binding legal advice; submission was not tested.
  • Client-portal promotion describes LOM as a platform for following cases, hearings, tasks, contracts, documents and appointments, and links to an external portal URL. Portal login or functionality was not inspected.
  • Team section includes a founder/director profile and nine named team cards; opening one card displays an individual bio modal. The team list includes job titles and ‘Arabic text Arabic text Arabic text’ actions.
