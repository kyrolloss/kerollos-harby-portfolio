NexGen Academy

Learn markets. Build your next skill.

Visit the live project ↗Source website · captured 2026-10-25

WHAT IT IS

Online forex-trading education academy offering courses, learning paths, and paid trading-support services.

Presents forex and financial-market education through course listings and individual course pages, plus add-on services such as economic analysis. The public pages describe course topics, levels, durations, ratings, prices, learning outcomes and support features, and provide contact and start/enrollment prompts.

This case study documents what the linked website presents. Individual contribution to this project has not been confirmed.

WHAT THE WEBSITE OFFERS

  • The homepage promotes expert-led online courses and learning at any time/place; visible Arabic hero copy describes mastering new skills and expert-led courses.
  • The catalog page presents course, learning-path and services tabs, a search input, and category labels for financial markets, fitness, projects and digital marketing. Search/filter behavior was not tested.
  • Course catalog cards visibly show course titles, levels, durations, instructor names, ratings and prices; examples include Introduction to Forex (beginner, 3 hours, 4.9/77, free) and Chart Analysis (intermediate, 23 hours, 4.8/52, $600).
  • The Introduction to Forex detail page lists learning outcomes, target learners, prerequisites and a follow-on Chart Analysis course. It also advertises video, quizzes, mobile access, lifetime access and a completion certificate.
  • The Services catalog lists Advanced Economic Analysis, Smart Liquidity Services and Technical Analysis Services with 90-day access and displayed sale prices; the advanced-economic-analysis detail page lists live sessions, weekly reports, interactive chat and a private discussion group.
  • The About page states a mission focused on equipping people to navigate the forex market; the page displays learner/course/ratings metrics, presented as site claims rather than independently verified results.
  • Homepage and catalog pages show FAQ questions about trading, time to proficiency, course completion and profitability, plus links to contact and Telegram support.
  • A public Contact page displays a name/email/message form and direct email and Telegram contact options; no form was submitted.

VISITOR JOURNEY

From the homepage navigation, visitors can open About, Courses, Contact and Blogs; the supplied root URL resolves to the Arabic homepage at /ar.
