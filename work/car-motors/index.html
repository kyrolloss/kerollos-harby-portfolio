Brand name clarification

The supplied project label was “Car Motors”; the linked website brands itself as Cairo Motors. This page uses the visible on-site brand.

WHAT IT IS

New-car dealership / vehicle catalog and sales inquiries

An Arabic-language car dealership-style site presents a catalog of new vehicles with prices and monthly payment examples, links to a vehicle-detail page, and contact methods plus an inquiry form. These are visible site claims and interfaces; no backend sale, financing approval, booking, or form processing was verified.

This case study documents what the linked website presents. Individual contribution to this project has not been confirmed.

WHAT THE WEBSITE OFFERS

  • Homepage inventory cards display vehicle name, model year, availability, review-count labels, fuel type, seats, horsepower, transmission, price, and a monthly-payment example; the page states 'Arabic text Arabic text Arabic text 20 Arabic text' (20 cars found).
  • At least one inventory card links to a distinct vehicle detail route: Toyota Camry 2024 at /cars/1.
  • The Toyota Camry detail page visibly shows a three-image gallery with thumbnail choices and a 1/3 indicator.
  • The Camry detail page lists year, mileage, body type, fuel, engine, horsepower, seating, transmission, price, and feature labels including ABS, Airbags, lane tracking, distance control, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto.
  • The Camry page displays three example financing terms (36, 48, and 60 months), each with down-payment and monthly-payment figures.
  • The home page has a car-search text input and an 'Arabic text Arabic text' (Show filters) button; filtering behavior was not tested.
  • The contact section displays two phone numbers, two email addresses, a location, and a labeled inquiry form with name, email, phone, optional vehicle, message, and send button.
  • The header exposes section links for home, cars, about, and contact; a visible telephone link is also present. Link presence does not establish successful calling or processing.

VISITOR JOURNEY

Homepage hero 'Arabic text Arabic text' (Browse cars) links toward the inventory section; vehicle cards link to detail pages.
