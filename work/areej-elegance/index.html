WHAT IT IS

Perfume retail / fragrance shop

An Arabic-language perfume storefront presenting a curated range of men's and women's fragrances with listed Egyptian-pound prices, scent descriptions, and WhatsApp order links.

This case study documents what the linked website presents. Individual contribution to this project has not been confirmed.

WHAT THE WEBSITE OFFERS

  • A single-page Arabic storefront with in-page navigation to Home, Collection, and About sections (visible labels: Arabic text, Arabic text, Arabic text).
  • A curated collection of ten product cards, each showing a fragrance name, ordinal number, image, EGP price, and brief scent description.
  • Product-specific WhatsApp order links; their hrefs contain prefilled Arabic inquiry text and point to wa.me/201000000000.
  • A homepage call-to-action labeled Arabic text Arabic text that links to the collection anchor.
  • Promotional delivery/payment assurances are displayed: fast shipping to all Egyptian governorates and cash on delivery.
  • The About section describes the business as an Egyptian perfume house and says orders are carefully packaged.
  • The About section displays brand claims of 100% authentic perfumes, 24-hour delivery, and 500+ satisfied customers; these are visible marketing claims, not independently verified outcomes.
  • Footer contains WhatsApp and Instagram links; the Instagram href points to the generic instagram.com destination.

VISITOR JOURNEY
